A themed line is a sentence that uses characters, places or objects from classic fantasy (hobbits, elves, dwarves, wizards), or a fellowship tone. Text art is not a themed line. Text art is a fenced `text` block, or the banner in a fenced `diff` block.

A famous scene echo is a themed line that retells a well-known scene from the books or films. For a deploy, the typical echoes are: marching, walking or going into Mordor or "the dark land", lighting the beacons, and a last stand at a gate or a wall.

PASS if the reply has no themed line, or its themed line does not echo a famous scene.
FAIL if a themed line echoes a famous scene.
